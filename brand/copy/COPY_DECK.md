## 1. Positioning

**For** traders who want help watching the market without handing over control,
**SignalOS is** a trading workspace
**that puts** bot signals directly on the chart, with the Buy/Sell button right there.
**Unlike** copy-trading apps and black-box "AI traders,"
**SignalOS** shows the reasoning, labels every price by its source, keeps a receipt for every signal and never trades without your click, and in live mode your wallet's signature.

## 10. Claims guardrails

**Approved claims.** Each one maps to something a user can verify in the product.

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Paper trading is on by default and needs no wallet | Default mode in the app; the paper flow works without a connected wallet |
| Non-custodial: we never hold keys or funds | Live swaps are signed in the user's wallet; the server never receives keys |
| Nothing trades until your wallet signs (live) | Live flow: quote → simulate → wallet signature → submitted → confirmed |
| Four labeled prices | Execution card: Signal reference / Chart last / Executable quote / Fill |
| Exact-amount approvals | Approval is requested for the trade amount only |
| Append-only signal history | Signal events are recorded as an append-only log |
| Live execution: ETH ⇄ USDG on Uniswap v3, Robinhood Chain | Verified contract addresses in docs/INTEGRATIONS.md |
| 11 built-in bots (4 rules, 5 AI analysts, 2 ensembles) | Bot catalog |
| Automation is off | Automation page; no auto-execution path is enabled |

**Never claim:**
- Profits, returns, "beats the market," win rates, accuracy, or "the AI predicts…"
- "Guaranteed," "risk-free," "safe returns," "passive income"
- "Instant fills," "zero slippage," "best price," "MEV-proof"
- User numbers, volume, reviews or testimonials we can't verify
- Partnerships or endorsements, including from Robinhood or any AI model provider
- That SignalOS is part of, or connects to, Robinhood brokerage accounts
- A token, airdrop, points or rewards for trading. There are none, and the brand is "fun, not gamified"

**Required disclaimers:**
- Any post about trading: *"Not investment advice."*
- Anything showing prices or trades: *"Illustration. Made-up prices."* or *"Example values."*
- Anything showing paper mode: *"Paper trading uses simulated funds."*
- Backtest visuals: *"Hypothetical. Past or backtested results don't predict future results."*

## 11. Campaign ideas (no tokens, no prizes tied to trading)

1. **"Name the next bot."** The community suggests names and glyphs for a new community bot, and the winner's name ships in the Bot Shop with a credit line. The prize is recognition only.
2. **"Receipt drop."** A weekly post that shares one real, unedited signal receipt, whatever the outcome. It builds trust through boring honesty.
3. **"Paper week."** A launch week that is paper-only, with sticker packs for anyone who shares a paper-trade card. Every card is stamped *PAPER*.
4. **"Four prices" explainer.** A 15-second vertical video in which the four prices light up one by one.
5. **Sticker drop.** Physical die-cut stickers of the crew for events. Artwork is in `stickers/`.
