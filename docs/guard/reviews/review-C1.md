Do not ship either scoring table as written. Use B as the structural base, retain A's distinction between observations and qualified evidence, and replace the specific rules below. A underweights several immediately observable buyer risks. B overweights ordinary early distribution, then demands so much evidence of harmful selling that it can overlook the actual unloading campaign. Neither has demonstrated buyer usefulness or accuracy from 5.6 hours of replay.

## 1. Rating conventions and the first implementability failure

For the examples, assume supported routes, reconciled supply, complete required observations, released calibrated rules, no eligible history, no unmentioned authority/flow/integrity factor. If those assumptions fail, a below-High result becomes **Incomplete in A / Pending in B**, and buys are refused. Established High stays High with missing checks displayed. Today's measured dataset cannot satisfy those assumptions for most coins. Do not mistake the following candidate arithmetic for actual verified launch verdicts.

- A: `L/O/Au/X/I` means liquidity, ownership, authority, extraction, integrity. Within-family maximum, then sum; Lower <30, Elevated 30–69, High ≥70. A:255–278. History is separate.
- B: `E/O/Ff/C/I` means execution, ownership, flow, control, integrity. After mechanism allocation, family maximum, then sum; Lower <30, Caution 30–59, High ≥60. B:296–333.
- `S` is outstanding total supply; `F` is external liquid holder float. `R` is the worse valid reference-size round-trip loss. A uses 10% sell execution-discount depth; B uses 2% marginal-price depth. Those are different measurements. Cases specify both where they score; never substitute one number for the other.
- Benign identity does not imply low buyer risk. Some legitimate archetypes properly receive High. The false positive is an unsupported risk mechanism or attribution, not merely an honest team receiving a warning.

**Ambiguity before any testing:** A:264 says “maximum deduplicated held share of operator-side, affiliated insiders, qualified bundles or early buyers.” Is that the largest component, aggregate bundle union, or union of all four cohorts? A:167 defines bundle union, but A:264 is not explicit. B:177/303 uses the largest coordinated group while B:305 scores the entire early cohort. Ten disjoint 9%-float groups can consequently escape B's group threshold even if they jointly dominate supply. Fix **C01** specifies separate aggregate and largest-component metrics.

## 5. Does this match a good trencher's dashboard today?

**No demonstrated parity. A's proposed card could be thinner in actionable current pressure; B's card is richer, but its band can be dominated by a denominator artifact. Neither can claim “at least as useful” from a fields list.** research-tools and research-codex-r3 §4 document GMGN on4663, Codex analytics, GoPlus limits and Bubblemaps beta. Axiom/TrenchBot raw holder and bundle practices are useful comparators; verified4663 execution/feature parity for Axiom or RugCheck is not established. RugCheck is documented here as Solana-facing. Do not turn cross-chain familiarity into imaginary local support.

A good trencher still sees or asks for:

1. **Who still has the launch bag, including transfer descendants?** Bought-versus-held, not just DEV=0; origin-linked wallet paths even before control is proved. B:174 exposes the right metric without a corresponding score rule. A:167 loses it from cohort scoring.
2. **Who is unloading right now?** Open episodes, net token sells, sell volume by cohort, recurring size/cadence, recipient swaps and graduation sell waves. An actual recent harmful bot exit matters independently of the creator. A's X excludes that; both wait on counterfactual harm labels.
3. **What can my account sell at my size now?** Both reference sizes, max order/wallet, contract-vs-EOA behavior, price impact, fee decay, price gap, tax/fee components and quote validity. One chip based on worst $1k is not the risk faced by a $100 buyer.
4. **Is the apparent audience independent?** Independent economic buyers/flow after repeated cycling, service and cross-wallet duplicates; unresolved synchronization candidates; holder growth from purchases versus airdrops. Neither defines a complete live card for effective organic flow.
5. **Where is the hidden capital recycling?** Non-holder funders/collectors, exchanges as time-scoped leads, traced cash routes across launches. A's24h reuse and B's1h collection break plausible recurrence. Strong attribution requires evidence; the absence of it must not produce an empty map.
6. **What changes during the next hour?** Graduation route, antisnipe decay, five-minute caps, unlock/cliff time, queued mint/tax/blacklist upgrade. Present balances alone omit impending supply release.
