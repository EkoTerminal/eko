# Robinhood Chain memecoin findings — 2026-10-02

**Scope:** Web research as of October 2, 2026. “Accessed” dates indicate documentation inspection, not an independently executed RPC/API query. Reported incidents and historical parameter reads remain **unverified independently**.

## 1. Pons v2 (`pons_v2`)

- **Identity:** V2 factory `0x7eD598BcEf8bd9Edd8C97A195C6d13f40801EC7e`. V1 starts directly in Uniswap v3; V2 uses a constant-product curve, then Uniswap v4. [Official repository, accessed 2026-10-02](https://github.com/ponsdotdev/pons-labs).
- **Curve:** Entire supply initially belongs to the curve. Pricing uses virtual quote reserves plus real reserves, excluding accrued fees; buys increase price, sells decrease it. Reserved tokens equal `supply × phantomQuote / (phantomQuote + graduationThreshold)`. The final buy is capped at the sellable allocation and excess input refunded. [Curve source, accessed 2026-10-02](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ponsdotdev/pons-labs/main/contractsV2/src/v2/PonsV2BondingCurve.sol).
- **Published parameters:** 1 billion tokens, phantom reserve **1.68 ETH**, graduation at **4.2 ETH of net real reserves**, launch fee **0.0005 ETH plus gas**, base trading fee **1%**. These are historical reads, not guaranteed October 2 constants. ERC-20 pairs have asset-specific thresholds. Read the launch’s own `graduationThreshold()` and factory configuration. [Alchemii, 2026-08-29](https://www.alchemii.io/blog/pons-launchpad-explained); [Bitquery, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/blockchain/robinhood/pons-api/).
- **Graduation liquidity:** Reserves seed a full-range v4 position; the NFT and excess launch tokens reside permanently in `PonsV2LaunchLocker`, with no withdrawal/arbitrary-call path. This is **locked, not burned or creator-owned**. Bitquery reports 5/7 of supply sold, 10/49 deposited as pool tokens, and 4/49 separately locked. Locked liquidity does not prevent holders dumping. [Official repository, accessed 2026-10-02](https://github.com/ponsdotdev/pons-labs); [Bitquery, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/blockchain/robinhood/pons-api/).
- **Creator tax/fees:** Optional creator tax is additional to the base fee, fixed at launch, charged on buys and sells, and paid entirely to the creator. Source ceilings: creator tax 10%, base fee 10%, combined ordinary fees 20%; these are ceilings, not defaults. Base fees split protocol/creator/optional buyback using the launch’s snapshot. Inspect `feeBps()`, `creatorTaxBps()` and `getLaunchFeePolicy(token)`; no universal payout percentage verified. Buybacks vest over five years rather than burning. [Factory source, accessed 2026-10-02](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ponsdotdev/pons-labs/main/contractsV2/src/v2/PonsV2LaunchFactory.sol); [Official repository, accessed 2026-10-02](https://github.com/ponsdotdev/pons-labs). Graduated pools have zero static LP fee; the hook charges fees. [Bitquery, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/blockchain/robinhood/pons-api/).
- **Anti-sniper tax:** Buy-only, starts at 99%, decays exponentially; receipts fold it into `CurveBuy.fee`. Official docs say **five seconds**, while Bitquery says **three**; September 7 example reads also show three. **Current window unresolved**: consult each curve’s snapshotted terms and `currentSnipeTaxBps(recipient)`. Tax joins distributed trading fees, not a burn. [Pons docs, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2); [Bitquery, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/blockchain/robinhood/pons-api/); [MCP example, 2026-09-07](https://github.com/ponsmcp/pons-mcp/blob/main/docs/TOOLS.md).
- **Exemptions:** Launching address and creator fee recipient are automatically exempt; creators can name further wallets at creation for team opening buys. Exemptions cannot be added later and are keyed to token recipient, not transaction sender. `SnipeTaxExempted(address)` logs in the launch receipt reveal them. Exemption signals privileged launch access, not proof of fraud. [Pons docs, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2); [Bitquery, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/blockchain/robinhood/pons-api/).

## 2. Other factories; official Stock Tokens

**LONG/long.xyz and Bankr** use Doppler Airlock `0xeb7c034704ef8dcd2d32324c1545f62fb4ad0862`; `LongLauncher` is `0x22e99278308b393ea1260859b181ad7e78f5eeed`. A shared Airlock does not uniquely identify the frontend: inspect launcher/call provenance. Bitquery also documents Flap, Bags, Klik, Ape.store, Clanker, pools.trade and trench.today. [Factory guide, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/blockchain/robinhood/robinhood-meme-coin-launches/).

Bankr explicitly supports `chain: "robinhood"`/4663, WETH and stock-token pairings. Its documented Doppler fee is 0.7%, with 95% to the creator (0.665% of volume). These are Bankr terms, not Pons terms. [Deploy docs, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bankr.bot/token-launching/api-reference/deploy-token-launch/); [Fee FAQ, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.bankr.bot/faq/token-launching/).

Official Stock Tokens are ERC-20 tokenised debt securities issued by **Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited**, providing economic exposure without underlying-share ownership rights. Verify exact `(4663, contract address)` against the canonical registry or `/rhj/assets` deployments. Names/tickers and stock-paired memecoin pools do not establish official issuance. [Stock Tokens](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/), [canonical contracts](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/contracts/), [asset API](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/) — accessed 2026-10-02.

## 3. Public investigations

**53-launch ring:** Crowdfund Insider (September 30), reporting Wazz’s September 27 investigation, alleges **$18.43m extracted** across roughly two months, mostly Pons V2. Bundles of 70–200 wallets reportedly captured ≥70% of supply within one second. Linking: 45 launches connected by collector→next-funder transfers; four shared a batch-funding key/address; four shared a collector. These categories may overlap—do not sum them. Example: DRAFT wallets swept 179.88 ETH to a hub; hub→hop→DEED funder transferred 20 ETH, followed 16 seconds later by DEED’s funding batch. Largest reported extractions: CRUMBS $3.12m, LEGS $2.9m, PINK $1.44m. Two further clusters were excluded, with no totals provided. **Allegations, not independently reproduced or adjudicated; public transactions cannot prove possession of a private key.** [Crowdfund Insider, 2026-09-30](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/09/313755-robinhood-chain-memecoins-to-18-43m-rug-pull-ring-analysis/).

**ARROW/CASHDOG:** Bubblemaps reported 200 ARROW wallets sharing funder `0x0a5…6a6c`, no prior EVM activity, and buys within three minutes; those plus other clusters held 80%. CASHDOG used one-time contracts funding holders. Its top-50 analysis counted 164,538 trading addresses, 63% losing/37% profitable; methodology is insufficient to reproduce P&L. These are concentration analyses, not independently proven rugs. [Bubblemaps, 2026-07-23](https://blog.bubblemaps.io/robinhood-trenches-11m-gone-and-164k-traders-later/).

## 5. Timing and ordering

Official policy is **FCFS by arrival at the sequencer**, with higher fees unable to bypass earlier transactions. GMGN reports approximately **100ms blocks**; not independently measured today. Chainstack’s July 27 sample found empty Timeboost metadata in 550/550 messages, supporting absence then; **October 2 configuration unverified**. [Robinhood docs, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/); [GMGN, 2026-09-07](https://gmgn.ai/blog/how-to-buy-new-robinhood-chain-launches/); [Chainstack measurement, 2026-07-27](https://github.com/chainstacklabs/robinhood-chain-sequencer-feed).

**Implication:** Latency still matters; same-block buys alone do not prove coordinated control. Pons’s launch-and-buy router executes its initial purchase inside the creation transaction, giving structural precedence over subsequent public buys. “Bundle” need not mean a protocol-guaranteed multi-transaction bundle. [Router docs, accessed 2026-10-02](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2).
