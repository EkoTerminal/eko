## 2. Evidence of prediction versus retrospective detection

- **Catching the Rug, 2608.20271:** first **5 minutes**, including first-three-block price movement, trade counts, unique buyers/sellers, buy/sell ratios and volatility, predict **one-hour** outcome: **99% TVL drawdown OR 80% inactivity**. Already-rugged five-minute cases are excluded. Fusion→Pump.fun XGBoost reports **F1 0.7885, MCC 0.3947, PR-AUC 0.8011**; separate precision/recall and feature importances are not reported. Cross-platform MCC can be negative. **Caveat:** Table V platform counts appear reversed relative to Table II; inactivity is not proof of extraction or buyer loss. [Paper, §§IV–VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20271v1).

- **MELT, 2602.13480v2:** features use the **whole pre-migration sale**, not fixed first 1–5 minutes. High-risk label: minimum price/migration price **<0.3 within 20 minutes OR manually identified manipulation**; low requires **≥0.7 and no manipulation**. MLP high-risk **precision 0.8459, recall 0.8325**. Ablation AUPRC **0.5729 →0.5369** without market activity, **0.5451** without bundles, **0.5693** without raw concentration: activity and concealed ownership add predictive information. Simulated migration-entry, random exits over the next hour: top-100 MLP loss **26.64% versus 60.71%** random. Caveats: migration-only population, filters exclude <1-minute sales/<100 holders; v2 uses chronological splitting; table AUPRC’s positive-class interpretation is unclear. [Paper, §§3–4 and tables 8–10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.13480v2).

- **SolRugDetector, 2603.24625:** retrospective rule detection: executed freezes, creator liquidity-removal profit followed by inactivity, or persistent pool-balance/holder decline (**73% holder reduction**); activity cutoff **5 trades/hour**. On **117 reported rugs +65 verified controls**, precision **100%**, recall **93.16%**. No learned importances or first-five-minute forecast: post-event evidence cannot establish early predictive power. [Paper, §§III–IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24625v1).

- **Midsummer Meme’s Dream, 2507.01963:** extraction labels include **>99% daily price collapse plus >99% sustained volume collapse**, and separately pump/dump rules. **62.9%** of extraction-affected tokens previously showed wash trading/LPI. This is retrospective association in selected high-return tokens, not prospective precision/recall or a first-block model; no predictive feature importances. [Paper, §§5–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01963v1).

- **Meme Coin Factories, 2609.10246:** detects wash trades, shared-funder creators and coordinated liquidation; strict dump label requires multiple senders and sale **within one transaction**. Reports **4,402 dumps totaling 33,393 SOL** in the 1% sample. Strong extraction-mechanism evidence, but no buyer-loss forecasting model, importances or measured predictive precision/recall. [Paper, §§4–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10246v1).

- **Additional 2026 evidence, 2603.13830:** BSC wash-pattern RF **precision 0.9286, recall 0.6190**; removing trade/address features reduces PR-AUC by **0.1843/0.0573**. Only **seven tokens**; weak high-risk labels merge unknowns into non-high-risk; earliest **5,000 records**, not fixed minutes. Useful pilot, insufficient calibration evidence. [Paper, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13830v1).

- **Additional 2025 evidence, TON, 2509.01168:** first **five minutes →60-minute horizon**, separately modeling TVL collapse and inactivity. Fused-data Ston.Fi TVL model: **precision 0.74, recall 0.81, AUC 0.891**. Important features: five-minute maximum TVL and creation/trade time gaps; inactivity models emphasize volume and buy counts. Importance ranking is reported without numeric weights in the prose. Chronological splitting is not specified. [Paper, §§4–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.01168v1).

## 3. Wording and disclaimers

Solana Tracker calls scores “algorithmic assessments only,” disclaims safety guarantees, and limits information to informational use. Token Sniffer uses **low/medium/high rug-pull risk**. GMGN instead says **buyable / buyable with conditions / not advised / do not buy**, explaining this concerns exitability rather than appreciation—still recommendation-like wording. These are observed practices, not proof of legal protection. [Tracker terms](https://www.solanatracker.io/terms), [Sniffer](https://tokensniffer.readme.io/reference/response), [GMGN](https://github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills/blob/main/skills/gmgn-contract-dd/SKILL.md).

## What a calibrated buyer-risk score should include, with evidence

**Proposed design:** define loss from an executable entry price over a stated horizon; distinguish catastrophic loss, inability to exit and insider extraction. Prioritize early participation/flow, liquidity dynamics and **cluster-adjusted early ownership**; MELT supports the latter beyond raw holder counts, while Catching supports five-minute market features. Keep observed sell blocks/freeze execution as explicit critical flags. [MELT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.13480v2), [Catching](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20271v1), [SolRugDetector](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24625v1).

**Proposed calibration:** prevent correlated ownership flags from being counted repeatedly; validate family interactions instead of inventing bonuses. Preserve unknowns and data age, publish coverage, test future launches by platform and creator cluster, and report precision/recall plus realized loss per band. No cited vendor threshold establishes a calibrated loss probability. Render **LOWER OBSERVED RISK / CAUTION / HIGH RISK / INSUFFICIENT DATA**, with horizon, reasons and “not a buy recommendation; lower risk does not guarantee safety.” [Missing-data precedents](https://docs.gopluslabs.io/reference/response-details), [Platform shift](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20271v1), [Disclaimer precedent](https://www.solanatracker.io/terms).
