## Implementation and validation

The original landing inherited approximately 63 kB gzip of shared schema initialization through disclosure copy, scan validation and eager telemetry. Disclosure constants now have a lightweight shared export; examples validate after a rendering opportunity, lower panels load on intersection, scan helpers load on submission, and error telemetry loads on demand. All original validation remains. The wallet provider, query cache, connector discovery and transaction helpers load on wallet actions or wallet-dependent routes. The App shell and its realtime effects stay mounted across activation. Pending-hash reporting is separated from signing helpers. ABI helpers have their own deferred vendor chunk so the largest chunk meets the unchanged 80 kB cap.

Examples reserve the actual 44 px touch-button height, counter captions reserve their text height, and deferred landing panels have a placeholder. The service worker installs only the static public shell JS; wallet/chart/routes remain cacheable on use. Landing arrival does not register the terminal worker. The build gate also checks precache membership, and worker tests verify deferred assets are absent at install and cached when requested. Warm approval readiness remains unmeasured, not a pass.
