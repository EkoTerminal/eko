# UX audit: ekoterminal.com, first-time visitor (2026-10-04)

Scope: the landing at `/` and the terminal (Radar, New pairs, Feed, Bags, Watchlist, Mission Control, Connect an agent,
Scoreboard, Census, coin view, `/scan`, Drops) at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 (touch, coarse pointer), with and without
`prefers-reduced-motion`. Live trading is off on staging. Screenshots are in [`ux-audit-2026-10-04/`](ux-audit-2026-10-04/);
`01`–`22` are the live site before this branch, `30`–`39` are this branch's build reading the same public staging API.

## Summary: top issues

1. **Blocker · landing:** the first ~29 screens (23 on phones) are a text-free scroll animation, and the marketing page is not even mounted until it ends. Reduced-motion users get the same gate. No EKO name, chain or way in within five seconds.
2. **Blocker · landing (fixed):** outside a sample screenshot, the landing never named Robinhood Chain, Pons or Uniswap. The only chain in its copy was Base (a planned Drop 8). Hero, FAQ, footer and meta now say it.
3. **Major · terminal header (fixed on desktop):** "LIVE · BLOCK n" with no chain name on any page. It now leads with "Robinhood Chain" (chain ID 4663 in the tooltip). Phones still have no chain context in the top bar.
4. **Major · Radar and New pairs (partly fixed):** every row carries "Legacy assessment · rules 1.0.x" and "Guard 2 assessment unavailable", unexplained. A one-line legend and tooltips now explain both. The rows are still three lines tall.
5. **Major · coin view (fixed):** every coin page showed "Cannot reach EKO. Approvals need a connection." because the paused trade quote returns a 503.
6. **Major · trust:** "LIVE · updated 0s ago" while the header block jumps about 108k blocks (about 3 h) between page loads and Guard snapshots are 3.4 h old. Related to the known stale-updates issue.
7. **Major · Scoreboard:** the public record reads as broken: "Unavailable" in 48 px mono, "Monitoring is unavailable." three times, and 100 raw rows of ISO timestamps and full addresses. The missing page gutter is fixed.
8. **Major · Mission Control (fixed):** signed-out visitors saw an error with a pointless Retry button, and Connect an agent had no title or purpose.
9. **Major · Feed (fixed):** coins with no symbol showed a bare "$", times were in local time beside a UTC header, and the counter said "1 events". Still open: WETH and USDG appear as "New pair created".
10. **Major · data consistency:** Radar says a coin's market cap is $94K and its coin page says $23K. The coin chart rises while the header shows −19% (1h). Trade buttons look live while trading is paused.

## Method

- Headless Chromium against the public site, 14 routes × 2 widths. Captured for each: an early and a settled screenshot, visible text, headings and landmarks, the first 25 tab stops with focus-ring detection, unnamed controls, target sizes, an approximate contrast check (text on solid backgrounds), console errors and requests.
- The landing was captured at intro 0%, 50% and 90%, and then section by section after "Skip intro".
- The fixes were checked in the in-app browser against a local build that read the public staging API (GET only, writes blocked), at 1440 px and 390 px.

## Findings

Severity: **blocker** stops a first-time visitor understanding or using the site; **major** misleads or blocks a key task;
**minor** is polish. Status: **Fixed** (this branch), **Partly fixed**, **Rec** (a recommendation), **Owner** (needs a decision).

### Blockers

**B1 · Landing intro gates the whole landing** · `/` desktop and mobile · `01`, `02` · Rec (the intro is owned by another session)
- What happens: `IntroGate` renders nothing but the intro until it finishes, over a runway of 29 screens (23 on phones). The intro has no headings, no links and no EKO name. The only way out is "Skip intro": a 10 px label (9 px on phones), 86 × 34 px, top right. Under reduced motion it holds a still frame but still needs the full scroll to finish.
- Why it matters: the owner's "not really apparent what chains" starts here. The visitor sees "DISCOVER" and "SCROLL 00%", nothing else. Crawlers and link unfurls also get an empty page.
- Recommendation:
  - Mount the landing under the intro, not after it.
  - Skip the intro entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion` and for returning visitors (a session flag).
  - Put the wordmark, "A trench terminal for Robinhood Chain" and an "Open terminal" link in the intro's HUD from frame 0.
  - Cut the runway to six screens or fewer, and make "Skip intro" a 44 px target with 12 px or larger text.

**B2 · The landing never said which chain** · `/` hero, FAQ, footer, `<meta>` · `03`, `04` → `30`, `31` · **Fixed**
- What happened: the hero said "Look beyond the price. Follow wallet activity, market context and the reasons behind each signal." Robinhood Chain appeared only inside the sample-dashboard image, and Pons and Uniswap never appeared at all. The only chain named in the copy was "Base Expansion" (Drop 8). The footer had the non-affiliation line without "Built on Robinhood Chain", so it read as a disclaimer about something the page never mentioned.
- Fix:
  - Hero (desktop and mobile): "A trench terminal for Robinhood Chain. Every new Pons launch and Uniswap pool, scanned for scam playbooks."
  - FAQ "What is EKO?" adds the first application, in the canonical promise's words.
  - Footer: "Built on Robinhood Chain · Not affiliated with…", and the full D-1 text "DYOR · Not financial advice · AI-generated analysis".
  - Meta description: the approved bio line (02-MARKETING §05).
  - The hero CTA "Explore EKO" is now "Open terminal".

### Major

**M1 · No chain context in the terminal header** · every terminal page · `05`, `07`, `09` → `32` · **Partly fixed**
- The header showed "LIVE · BLOCK 79,858,561 · 13:21:10 UTC". It now leads with **ROBINHOOD CHAIN**; the tooltip reads "Robinhood Chain · chain ID 4663".
- Below 1120 px the label hides to protect the header layout, and the sidebar footer keeps "Built on Robinhood Chain".
- Rec: on phones (`06`, `20`) the top bar shows only the logo, plan chip and wallet. Add a chain caption under the wordmark, or a chain line at the top of the More sheet.

**M2 · Guard disclosures read as errors** · Radar, New pairs, `/scan` preview, coin view · `05`, `07`, `21` → `32`, `34` · **Partly fixed**, Owner
- What happens: each Radar row stacks "Legacy assessment · rules 1.0.x", the verdict chip and "Guard 2 assessment unavailable": three lines, identical on every row (about 95 px row height). Pair cards carry three tags. Nothing says what "legacy", "rules 1.0.x" or "Guard 2" mean, or what the visitor can do.
- Fix: one legend under the table's "not checked yet" note on Radar and New pairs, shown only while rows carry those labels: "Legacy assessment · rules 1.0.x: the grade from EKO's first rule set, shown for reference. Guard 2 assessment unavailable: the current buyer-risk check has no result for this coin yet, and buys stay unavailable until it does." Both tags also get matching tooltips. The disclosure text on every row is unchanged.
- Owner: collapsing the two labels into one compact "Legacy grade · Guard 2 pending" chip would halve row height, keeping the full text in the tooltip and inspector. That changes how the Guard 2 disclosure is presented, so it needs a compliance call.

**M3 · False "Cannot reach EKO" banner on every coin page** · `/coin/:address` desktop · `19` → `39` · **Fixed**
- What happened: with live trading off, `POST /v1/trade/quote` answers `503 {"error":"sim_unavailable"}`. The API client treated any 5xx as "server unreachable" and raised the global banner above the header: "Cannot reach EKO. Approvals need a connection." A visitor on a public page, with no approvals, read it as an outage.
- Fix: a 5xx carrying a typed product error (`sim_unavailable`, `trading_paused`, `stale_data`…) comes from a reachable server and no longer raises the banner. Bare 5xx and `internal_error` still do.

**M4 · "Live" while the data is hours old** · header on every page, Radar, coin view · `05` vs `12` and `13`; `19` · Rec
- What happens:
  - The header block comes from the highest block seen in Feed events. Different page loads showed 79,858,561 and 79,966,670 ten seconds apart: about 108k blocks, or about 3 h at roughly 100 ms per block.
  - The coin view's Guard section says "As of block 79,858,478 · 12327 s ago".
  - Radar still says "Live · updated 0s ago".
- Why it matters: a terminal that says Live with a 3-hour-old guard snapshot undermines the "receipts, not hype" positioning.
- Recommendation:
  - Take the header block from the indexer head, not from the maximum Feed event.
  - Show "Behind ~3 h" (amber) when the head lags.
  - Apply the spec's `StaleBadge` (03-FRONTEND §3.0) to Guard snapshots older than the threshold.
  - Write ages in words ("3 h 25 m ago", not "12327 s ago").

**M5 · The public record reads as broken** · Scoreboard, `/scan` counters, Radar head · `16`, `17`, `21`, `05` → `38` · **Partly fixed**
- What happens:
  - Both counters say **Unavailable** in 48 px mono, and "Monitoring is unavailable." appears three times.
  - Calls lists 100 rows such as "2026-10-04T13:22:29.051Z · 0xccfb…aca7 (full) · pending · Grade: n/a · grade unavailable · call".
  - Radar's head shows the same metric as "— not checked yet".
  - The page had no gutter: the content touched the sidebar on desktop and the screen edge on phones.
- Fix: Scoreboard and Receipt now use the standard page frame (`shell-page`): 32 px gutter on desktop, 18 px on phones, and the display-face title.
- Recommendation:
  - Replace the two "Unavailable" figures with one explanation card: when counting starts, and why (no graded outcomes are accepted yet).
  - Use one phrase for missing values everywhere; "not checked yet" is already the spec's wording.
  - Format timestamps (UTC, short) and show coin symbols with short addresses.
  - Collapse repeated pending calls per coin.

**M6 · Mission Control asks to Retry when you are simply signed out** · `/mission`, `/mission/connect` · `13`, `14`, `15` → `36`, `37` · **Fixed**, Rec
- What happened: `/mission` showed "Verify your wallet to manage agents." with a **Retry** button (the 401 again), then a second card saying "Connect and verify your wallet to use this page." `/mission/connect` had no title, no heading and no purpose; the card butted against the header.
- Fix:
  - Signed out, Mission Control shows its title and purpose line, then one connect card.
  - Gated Mission pages (Connect an agent, agent detail, Approvals) render an eyebrow, the page title and one line on what the page is for.
- Rec: let signed-out visitors read the Connect steps (Claude Code pack, generic MCP). The instructions are the pitch for the harness, and creating a key is the only step that needs a wallet.

**M7 · Feed rows look broken** · `/feed` · `09`, `10` → `35` · **Partly fixed**
- What happened:
  - Coins without an indexed symbol rendered as a bare "$", on about half the rows.
  - Row times were the browser's local time ("09:21:30"), next to a header clock in UTC ("13:21:50 UTC").
  - The counter read "1 events in the last minute".
- Fix: such rows show a short address instead (`0x9d7e…8ef3`), Feed times are in UTC under a "Time (UTC)" header (matching the coin view's trades table), and the counter says "1 event".
- Rec:
  - "$WETH" and "$USDG" appear as "New pair created" and top Busiest coins. The row is naming the quote asset instead of the new token, so show the base token.
  - Each Feed load also fires about 12 `GET /v1/coins/:addr` calls that return 404 (console errors) for unindexed coins. Skip those lookups.
  - The kind filters offer Trades, Ghost Reports, Swarm calls and Burns, all ticked, although the note above says they aren't tracked yet. Disable them with the note as their tooltip.

**M8 · Numbers that disagree** · Radar inspector vs coin view · `19`, `20` · Rec
- What happens:
  - For `$SI`, Radar's `marketCapUsd` is $94.1K and the coin view says "MCap $23K", because the coin view computes price × circulating supply.
  - The coin chart draws a rising line up to about $0.00015 while the header says $0.0000941 and −19.4% (1h).
  - Chart axes use local time; the header and trades table use UTC.
- Recommendation:
  - Label FDV and circulating market cap explicitly, and use the same one on Radar and the coin view.
  - Check the candle source against the price on the curve.
  - Use UTC on chart axes, or label the zone.

**M9 · Trade buttons look live while trading is paused** · Radar, New pairs, coin view · `05`, `07`, `19` · Rec
- What happens:
  - Radar rows and Near graduation cards show the primary **Trade** button, although `/v1/config` has `trading.liveEnabled: false`.
  - The coin trade panel stacks "Data is stale — trading paused.", "Live trading is paused. Scans and quotes still work." twice, and "Retry quote".
  - The custom amount field reads "100" while the $25 chip is selected and "Max $25 per trade during launch." is printed below.
- Recommendation:
  - While live trading is off, show a secondary "Quote" button (or a disabled Trade button with the tooltip "Live trading is off on staging").
  - Print the paused state once.
  - Keep the custom amount in sync with the selected chip and the cap.

**M10 · The way from the landing into the terminal, and back** · `/`, terminal brand link · `03` · **Partly fixed**, Rec
- What happened: the landing's buttons said "Explore EKO" and "Discover" (both vague), and the story never links to `/scan`, though 03-FRONTEND §2.2 says it should. In the terminal, the brand link goes back to `/`, which replays the 29-screen intro (B1).
- Fix: "Explore EKO" is now **Open terminal** (hero and nav).
- Rec: add a "Scan a coin" link to `/scan` in the landing nav and hero. Returning from the terminal should not replay the intro.

**M11 · Disclaimer repetition on the coin view** · `/coin/:address` · `19` · Rec
- "Buyer risk at this snapshot. Not a buy recommendation." appears 8 times on one page, and the full DYOR / Built on / non-affiliation / policy-links block 4–5 times.
- Repetition turns required text into noise people skip.
- Keep the spec placements (03-FRONTEND §8: the coin card footer and the trade panel's fine print) once each.

**M12 · Mobile filter rows are cut off without an affordance** · Radar, Feed (390 px) · `06`, `10` · Rec

- Recommendation: wrap the segmented controls on phones, or add an edge fade and scroll snap.

**M13 · Head stats with no data** · New pairs, Feed, Radar heads · `07`, `08`, `09` · Rec
- What happens: New pairs shows "— new pairs today", "— median first scan" and a red **Danger** chip followed by "— refused at launch". The chip, used as a label, looks like a button. Feed shows "— Ghost Reports in the Feed"; Radar shows "— not checked yet · Honeypots refused".

## Trust and claims

- **Forbidden terms:** a scan of every captured page found none in EKO's own copy: no "audited", "trustless", "automated burn", "ownerless", "guaranteed", "win rate" or "rug-proof", and no price predictions. "Hood" appears only inside third-party token names (for example "Green Hood"), which are untrusted text.
- **"Safe" (Owner):** "Safe" appears as the risk-mode name (Safe / Balanced / Degen) in the sidebar and trade panel. 03-FRONTEND §2.1 and §3.6 name the mode that way, but AGENTS.md hard rule 3 lists "safe" as forbidden user-facing copy. Rec: rename it (for example "Careful / Balanced / Degen"), or confirm that the mode name is exempt.
- **Robinhood wording:**
  - Every Robinhood mention found is "Built on Robinhood Chain", "Robinhood-connected agent" or the full non-affiliation line.
  - The sidebar footer (desktop) and the More sheet (mobile) carry `BUILT_ON` and `NON_AFFILIATION`.
  - The landing footer now carries both lines too (B2).
- **D-1 (DYOR):** present on Radar, Feed, coin view, `/scan` and Census. The landing's version was shortened to "DYOR · Not financial advice."; it is now the full text.
- **Guard 2 disclosures:** kept on every row; this branch only adds explanations (M2).

## Accessibility basics

- **Focus order:**
  - On terminal pages: skip link, brand link, search, then the 25 sidebar stops, then the page. The skip link makes this acceptable.
  - Every focused element showed a visible ring.
  - The landing intro is a modal dialog: focus cycles between "Skip intro" and the scroll region, and Esc also skips.
- **Screen-reader names:**
  - No unnamed buttons or links on any audited page.
  - Verdict chips carry words, Feed kind icons have hidden text, decorative SVGs are `aria-hidden`.
  - Mobile tab-bar items have labels.
- **Contrast:** the automated check found no text under 4.5:1 on solid backgrounds. Text over the landing canvas and gradients was not measured.
- **Touch targets** (coarse pointer):
  - Primary controls are 44 px or more.
  - Exceptions: the brand link (71 × 22 px), the policy links in the More sheet and footers (14–16 px tall), and "Skip intro" (34 px tall).
  - On desktop, the sidebar's inline footer links are 12 px tall (inline-link exception, but they work as navigation).
- **Reduced motion:**
  - Terminal animations stop: Radar went from 12 running animations to 0, New pairs from 11 to 0.
  - The landing intro is the exception (B1).

## Visual consistency and density

- **Column widths:**
  - At 1440 px, Radar's table uses about 1000 px of the content area and drops Liquidity.
  - The coin column truncates names around 150 px: "Super Intelligence · …". The Radar launchpad tag sits on the symbol line so the age is not pushed into the ellipsis.
  - At 390 px, Radar keeps Coin, Guard and Exit as the spec asks. "EXIT AT $1K" truncates to "EXIT AT".
- **Page titles:** most terminal pages use the 52–76 px display face. Mission Control used a small bold sans; Scoreboard now matches the rest.
- **Page frame:** three wrapper conventions coexist: `page` with page-specific padding, `shell-page`, and `page trust-page` with no padding. Rec: one page frame component.
